About

What is Negros PowerWatch?

Negros PowerWatch is an independent, real-time electricity outage detection and alerting platform for Negros Oriental, Philippines.

We monitor actual power availability through multiple independent signals rather than relying exclusively on delayed official announcements.

How It Works

  1. Community members report power outages
  2. Our system correlates multiple independent reports
  3. Outages are detected automatically without waiting for official announcements
  4. When official sources confirm, we attach that information
  5. Restoration is detected when reports indicate power has returned

Privacy

We do not collect precise location data. Reports are anonymous and aggregated. No personal information is exposed on the public map.

Not Affiliated With

This project is independent and not affiliated with NORECO II, NGCP, or any government agency.
